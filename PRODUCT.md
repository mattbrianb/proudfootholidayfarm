# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Hosted on GitHub Pages (free plan) with a custom domain (www.proudfootholidayfarm.co.za, currently a Wix site being replaced). Booking tracker backend: Google Sheet + Google Apps Script web app (free), chosen because GitHub Pages cannot run server code.

## Users

- **Guests:** families, groups of friends and couples, mostly from KZN and Gauteng, planning a self-catering weekend or holiday, often with dogs. They browse on their phones, look at photos, check prices and dates, then make contact.
- **Owner (admin):** the family member who runs the farm. They manage bookings from their phone and are not technical.

## Product Purpose

Show the farm and its two places to stay well enough that a guest is ready to book, then get the guest in touch with the owner (WhatsApp, phone, email, enquiry form). Success is a steady flow of enquiries that come with clear dates and guest numbers.

## Positioning

A pet-friendly, fully off-grid farm stay 35 km from Mooi River (stated on the current site's home page) (borehole and spring water, solar power, gas for hot water and cooking) in Midlands bushveld between Mooi River and Greytown, in the Middlerus area near Craigie Burn Dam. It offers walking trails, birdlife, and off-road trails for quad bikes and 4x4s.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Two bookable places (Aloe Hill no longer exists and must not appear):

- **The Main House:** sleeps 15, for large groups.
- **The Cottage:** sleeps 2 and suits a couple, with room to add a mattress for little ones.

Rates (from the current site):
- R600 per adult per night, both places.
- R300 per child per night (ages 2–12).

Minimum numbers:
- Main House: 6 adults out of season, 10 in season (public holidays, long weekends, December).
- Cottage: 2 adults all year.

Booking terms:
- A 50% deposit secures a booking.
- Cancellation refunds of the deposit: 50% if more than 1 month before check-in, 25% if more than 2 weeks before, nothing within 2 weeks.

Pets: pet friendly within reason. R50 per dog per night (confirmed by the user, replacing the old site's conflicting "breakage fee over 3 dogs" wording).

Contact:

- Email: admin@proudfootholidayfarm.co.za
- Socials: Facebook (Proudfoot-holiday-farm-805416573182868) and Instagram (@proudfootholidayfarm).

Required features:

## Product Principles

1. Simple to click around: photos, prices, contact. Nothing gets in the way.
2. Each place stands on its own, with its own photos, details and availability.
3. Getting in touch takes one tap from anywhere.
4. The owner can run everything from their phone without touching code.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first. Pages must stay readable on rural mobile connections, so images have to be optimised.
