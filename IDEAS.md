# Ideas for later

These are possible additions to check with the owner first. None of them are built yet.

- **FAQ section.** Cover the questions guests always ask:
  - check-in and check-out times
  - what to bring (towels? linen? firewood?)
  - cell signal and Wi-Fi
  - whether the road suits low cars
  - braai wood
  - the nearest shops and fuel
  - what off-grid means day to day (for example, no hairdryers)
- **House rules and what's included.** List what's provided: linen, towels, kitchen equipment, braai grid, firewood and heating. Include the rules for dogs: beds, furniture and livestock.
- **Things to do nearby.** Craigie Burn Dam, Midlands Meander stops, fishing, hiking, Mooi River restaurants and Nottingham Road. This is useful for guests and helps the site show up on Google.
- **Seasonal story.** Snow in winter, green summers and spring aloes, using photos already on Instagram.
- **Special occasions page.** Birthdays, family reunions and small celebrations. The 70th birthday post on Instagram shows there's demand.
