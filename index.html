Life on the farm

Proudfoot sits in the bushveld of the Middlerus area, between Mooi River and Greytown, near Craigie Burn Dam.

More about the farm

  • Dogs welcomeBring the whole family, four legs included.
  • Completely off the gridSolar lights, borehole and spring water, and gas for hot water and cooking.
  • Trails and birdlifeWalking trails, quad and 4x4 tracks, and a big variety of birds.
  • Peace and quietOut of town, with nothing but hills and sky around you.
