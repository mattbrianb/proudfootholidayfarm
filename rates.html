Rates and policies

One simple price for both places. Here is everything you need to know before you book.

WhoPer night
Adults, the Main HouseR600
Adults, the CottageR600
Children aged 2 to 12, either placeR300
Dogs, per dogR50

Minimum numbers

  • The Main House: at least 6 adults out of season, and at least 10 in season (public holidays, long weekends and December).
  • The Cottage: 2 adults, in and out of season.

Deposit

A 50% deposit secures your booking. We will send banking details when we confirm your dates.

Cancellations

  • More than 1 month before check-in: 50% of the deposit refunded.
  • More than 2 weeks before check-in: 25% of the deposit refunded.
  • Less than 2 weeks before check-in: no refund.

Pets

We are pet friendly within reason. Dogs are R50 per dog per night. Please tell us how many dogs are coming when you enquire.
