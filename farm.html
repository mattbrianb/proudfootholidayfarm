The Farm

Bushveld, big skies and no power lines in sight.

Proudfoot is a holiday farm in the Middlerus area, between Mooi River and Greytown, near Craigie Burn Dam. It is about 35 km from Mooi River, far enough out of town to feel completely peaceful.

Completely off the grid

Our water comes from a borehole and running springs. Lights run off solar power and inverters, and hot water and the stoves run on gas. It is an eco-friendly way to stay, and it means wonderfully dark, starry nights.

Things to do

  • Walking trails across the farm
  • Birdwatching, with a big variety of birdlife
  • Spotting kudu, impala and other buck
  • Off-road trails for quad bikes and 4x4s
  • Braais under the stars
  • Play area with swings for the kids
  • Long walks with the dogs

What you'll find here

  • Free Wi-Fi

  • Cycling and mountain biking
  • Fishing
  • Personal safe
